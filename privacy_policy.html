1. Overview

Cooking Timer Bundle ("the app", "we", "our") is a standalone Wear OS application designed for Samsung Galaxy Watch. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, how it is stored, and what it is used for.

The short version: we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything the app records stays on your Galaxy Watch and is never sent anywhere.

🛡️

2. Data We Do Not Collect

🔒

any information to any server — including ours.


We do not collect, store remotely, or transmit any of the following:

✅

✅
No location data, GPS coordinates, or device location history.
✅

✅

The app stores the following information in a private local database on your Galaxy Watch. This data never leaves the device.

Data Type Purpose Storage Shared?
Custom timer names & durations Lets you reuse your own timers On-device only Never
Favourite presets Quick access to your starred timers On-device only Never

Deleting your data Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data from your watch. There is no cloud backup and no server copy to erase.
🔑

4. App Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions. Each one is used exclusively for the stated purpose — nothing more.

Permission Why it is needed
VIBRATE Haptic feedback when a timer finishes
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keeps the timer running when the watch screen turns off
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Required by Android for the cooking timer service type
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows the timer-complete alert on the watch
WAKE_LOCK Prevents the processor from sleeping mid-countdown
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores active timers if the watch is restarted

⚠️
No INTERNET permission The app does not request the INTERNET permission. Android enforces this at the OS level — without it, the app cannot make any network connection regardless of its own code.

6. Children's Privacy

Cooking Timer Bundle is rated 0+ (All Ages) and is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

Because the app collects no personal data of any kind, there are no special parental consent requirements. No account creation is required to use any feature of the app.

We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent international regulations.

⚖️

7. Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected or processed by us, standard data rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) apply only to the local data stored on your own device.

📱
Right to deletion You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app from your Galaxy Watch. This immediately and permanently removes the local database.
🔍
Right to access All data the app stores is visible directly within the app — in History, Favourites, Custom Timers, Meal Templates, and Settings.

Since we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or erase on our end.
